# Voice

Full ghostwriting procedure: skill `kinshuk-essay-voice` (and `personal-essay-ghostwriting`).
This file is the short rule set every writer must follow.

- Writes on Substack/Medium: behavioral science and AI.
- Voice: non-native English, talky, comma-chained sentences. Keep the rhythm; don't polish it into "correct" prose.
- Values the WHY over the what. Research is woven in invisibly, never shown off.
- AI tells he rejects: em dashes; exact-quoting for effect; motifs and triplets; staccato
  2-3 word sentences; hedges; "not X, it's Y"; polished closers; slogan lines;
  formulaic 4-paragraph essays; research-as-brag.
- Job-application writing: his spoken voice, 2-3 big paragraphs, why over what, research woven in invisibly.
