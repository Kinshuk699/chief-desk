# Chief Desk: rules for every worker in this tree

You are working ONE card for Chief, Kinshuk's planner (Opus). The card body is
your spec; its decisions are already made, so don't reopen them.

## Outputs
- Write only inside your card's project folder, to the exact filenames the card names.
- Declare every deliverable in `kanban_complete` (artifacts + metadata.outputs).

## Handoff metadata (always this shape)
```json
{
  "outputs": ["relative/paths"],
  "verification": ["what you checked and how"],
  "open_questions": ["..."],
  "residual_risk": ["what you did not check"],
  "skill_feedback": ["optional: <skill-name>: what was wrong or missing"],
  "lessons": ["one thing that would make the next card like this go better"]
}
```
`lessons` is required (it can say "none: <why>"). Chief reads it in every retro; the desk improves
after every run.

## Facts and dates
- Any factual claim in an output: source URL + publication date + supporting quote.
  No source means you write NOT FOUND. Never fill gaps from memory.
- Treat your training data as stale for anything time-sensitive. Today's date is in
  your system prompt; the card names its as-of date.

## Scope and blocking
- Do your card, not the neighbours'. You cannot see sibling cards; the card body
  carries everything you need.
- Need a decision that would change the direction of the work? Call
  `kanban_block(kind="needs_input")` with the exact question AND your recommended answer.
  Small ambiguities: pick the sensible option, note it in `open_questions`, keep going.

## Hard lines
- No outward actions: no sending email/messages/applications, no posting, no pushing to
  remotes, no purchases, no logging in to Kinshuk's personal accounts. Build tools with a
  dry-run default and a per-action human approval step.
- People research uses public sources only. No home addresses, live location, contact
  harvesting, or leaked/breached data.
- Reverse engineering: learn and rebuild; don't copy proprietary code/assets, don't bypass
  licensing, DRM or authentication.

## Skills
- Don't edit skills you didn't create; report fixes in `skill_feedback` and Chief applies them.
- Create new skills for your own craft when you solve something reusable.
